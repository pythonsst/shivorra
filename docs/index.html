Cue 02Method

How an engagement runs

In order, because the order is the point — nothing starts before the thing it depends on is settled.

  1. 01

    Call

    Forty-five minutes. You describe the product and the deadline; we tell you honestly whether we're the right team and what we'd be worried about.

  2. 02

    Scope

    A week of paid discovery. You leave with a written build plan, a fixed price, and a date — yours to keep whether or not you hire us.

  3. 03

    Build

    Two-week increments against a shared board. A working build on a real device at the end of every one, no exceptions and no demo-day theatre.

  4. 04

    Hand over

    Pipelines, docs, and a walkthrough with whoever inherits it. We stay on retainer if you want us; the code works if you don't.
